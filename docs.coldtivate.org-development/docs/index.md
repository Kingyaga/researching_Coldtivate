# Welcome to Coldtivate: Smart Cold Chain Management

Coldtivate is a mobile application designed to empower farmers and cooling operators with efficient storage solutions. The application facilitates the tracking of produce stored in cooling units and refrigerated trucks, and provides real-time monitoring of remaining storage life. The Coldtivate app has been developed as part of the Your Virtual Cold Chain Assistant project ([Your VCCA](https://yourvcca.org)) by the [BASE Foundation](https://energy-base.org/) and [Empa](https://www.empa.ch/web/simbiosys), with the support of partner software companies.

## Key Benefits

* **Optimize Storage:** Increase produce shelf life with real-time monitoring and predictive analytics.
* **Enhance Market Access:** Use market insights and price forecasts to make informed sales decisions.
* **Empower Knowledge:** Access educational resources and best practices for effective postharvest handling.
* **Improve Efficiency:** Streamline operations with smart notifications and decentralized cooling solutions.

## Key Features

### Storage Management

* Digitally track cold room inventory.
* Monitor storage conditions in real-time using sensor data.
* Simulate crop aging and predict Time to Pick Up (TTPU) with digital food twins.
* Integrate hygrothermal sensors for precise cold room monitoring.
* Share cold room occupancy, temperature, and revenue for transparent monitoring.

### Market Insights

* Visualize crops in storage and purchase directly from cooling users.
* Access price forecasts for key crops in India and Nigeria.
* Utilize tools to make informed sales decisions.

### User Experience

* Supports multiple languages, including English, French, Hindi, Arabic, and Hausa.
* Receive timely alerts via in-app notifications or SMS.
* Locate nearby cold storage facilities with an interactive map.
* Enjoy streamlined registration for farmers and operators.
* Choose from flexible registration options: direct signup or operator-assisted.

### Knowledge Resources

* Access a comprehensive repository of best practices and cold storage guidelines.
* Engage with educational comics about cooling benefits and business models.
* Explore a detailed knowledge base on postharvest handling techniques.

### Decentralized Cooling

* Access cost-effective cooling solutions through Cooling-as-a-Service.
